# Employment, workplace injury and public services

| Question | Facts, authority and route |
|---|---|
| Was the person an employee, and what pay or leave is owed? | Obtain the agreement, dates, actual hours, pay and leave records. For work from 21 February 2026, test every element of the specified-contractor exception in the Employment Relations Act 2000, s 6. If it does not apply, examine the real nature of the relationship. For an earlier period, use the law then in force. Compare the applicable minimum wage order and Holidays Act 2003 entitlement with the employer's calculation. For a collective agreement, check its coverage and the worker's union membership. [Employment status tests and commencement](https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/types-of-worker/employee-or-contractor) · [Employment records](https://www.employment.govt.nz/starting-employment/rights-and-responsibilities/record-keeping) · [Minimum wage rates and categories](https://www.employment.govt.nz/pay-and-hours/pay-and-wages/minimum-wage/minimum-wage-rates-and-types) |
| Can the employee challenge a dismissal or disadvantage? | Identify the grievance ground under s 103, effective dismissal date, notice, reason, process and any trial provision. Section 114 normally gives 90 days to raise the grievance with the employer, or 12 months for a sexual-harassment grievance under s 103(1)(d), starting when the action occurred or came to the employee's notice, whichever was later. For a late grievance check employer consent or leave under ss 114–115. A separate three-year period runs from raising the grievance to commencing proceedings. Mediation is available and the Employment Relations Authority can determine qualifying claims. [Section 114](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2000/24/en/latest/sections/DLM60354/) · [Employment New Zealand grievance guidance](https://www.employment.govt.nz/resolving-problems/how-to-resolve-problems/personal-grievances) |
| Does remuneration affect the dismissal claim? | For dismissals affected by the 2026 amendments, check statutory annual remuneration, the applicable threshold, the position held before 21 February 2026, later changes of position, and any written agreement about dismissal protections. Sections 67I–67J and 113A–113B concern unjustified dismissal and dismissal-related disadvantage. Other grounds, such as discrimination, require their own analysis. [Section 113A](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2000/24/en/latest/sections/LMS1575932/) · [Schedule 1AA, transitional provisions](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2000/24/en/latest/sections/DLM6407654/) · [Employment New Zealand on the threshold and transition](https://www.employment.govt.nz/ending-employment/dismissal/dismissal-rules-for-high-income-earners) |
| Who deals with a workplace injury? | Identify each business or undertaking with control over the risk, the sector regulator and whether the event was notifiable. Check the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, s 36 (primary duty) and ss 55–57 (preserve the site, notify and keep records). For a worker seeking treatment or weekly compensation, identify the ACC cover or entitlement decision. A formal review application normally falls due within three months of the decision letter. Check the late-application grounds in the Accident Compensation Act 2001, s 135. [WorkSafe on the statutory duties](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/getting-started/introduction-hswa-special-guide/) · [ACC review application](https://www.acc.co.nz/im-injured/resolve-an-issue/apply-for-a-review-acc33) |
| Can a benefit decision be reviewed? | Obtain the Ministry of Social Development (MSD) notice, notification date, payment and period, and reasons for refusing, reducing or recovering payment. For a reviewable delegated decision, the ordinary route is an application to MSD within three months after notification, with provision for a further period. If unresolved, a Benefits Review Committee reviews it. For an appeal within jurisdiction, the Social Security Appeal Authority usually requires filing within 60 working days after notification of the committee decision. Specified medical grounds go to the Medical Appeals Board. Check the Social Security Act 2018, ss 391–400 and 411 onwards. [Social Security Act, Part 7](https://legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/latest.pdf) · [Work and Income review procedure](https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/about-work-and-income/feedback-and-complaints/review-of-decisions.html) · [Medical appeals](https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/about-work-and-income/feedback-and-complaints/review-of-a-medical-decision.html) |
| Is the dispute about support funding, care or consent? | For a disability support allocation, obtain the assessment and funding plan and ask the Needs Assessment and Service Coordination provider or Enabling Good Lives site about review. For the conduct of a health or disability provider, check the Code of Health and Disability Services Consumers' Rights, particularly Rights 6 (information), 7 (choice and consent) and 10 (complaints). The provider, advocacy service or Health and Disability Commissioner can receive a complaint. For a treatment injury, separately assess ACC cover under s 32 of the Accident Compensation Act. [Disability Support Services assessment and review](https://www.disabilitysupport.govt.nz/disabled-people/assessment-and-funding/needs-assessment-services) · [Commissioner's Code of Rights](https://www.hdc.org.nz/your-rights/about-the-code/code-of-health-and-disability-services-consumers-rights/) |
| Who can decide whether a student returns to school? | For a domestic student at a state school, identify the student's age, the principal's written decision, the notice to the family and the school calendar. Under the Education and Training Act 2020, ss 80–83, a stand-down, suspension, exclusion and expulsion have different decision-makers and consequences. For a suspension, check the board's meeting deadline and opportunity for the family to be heard. For a complaint about a state school board's administration, check the Ombudsman's jurisdiction. [Education and Training Act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/38/en/latest/) · [Ministry guidance on the suspension meeting](https://www.education.govt.nz/education-professionals/schools-year-0-13/administration-and-management/guidelines-for-principals-and-school-boards-on-stand-downs-suspensions-exclusions-and-expulsions/suspension-meetings/timing-of-the-suspension-meeting) · [Ombudsman complaints](https://www.ombudsman.parliament.nz/make-complaint/complain-about-action-or-decision-agency) |

**Citation verification example.** A worker notified WorkSafe about a fall and later received letters from ACC declining weekly compensation and from MSD reducing a benefit. Obtain both letters and their dates. The WorkSafe report establishes when the safety issue was raised. The ACC decision requires its own review application, while the MSD reasons determine whether the benefit dispute belongs before a Benefits Review Committee or the Medical Appeals Board. Record each deadline against its own notice. [ACC review application](https://www.acc.co.nz/im-injured/resolve-an-issue/apply-for-a-review-acc33) · [Work and Income review procedure](https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/about-work-and-income/feedback-and-complaints/review-of-decisions.html)
