# Guide to reviewing New Zealand legal questions and answers

Start with the legal consequence the user wants. A shopper seeking a refund, a worker challenging a dismissal and an injured patient seeking compensation need different authorities and different decision-makers. Record where the relevant events occurred, who made the decision or supplied the goods, the dates of the events and notices, and the remedy sought.

## When does location change the answer?

| Question | Facts that select the authority |
|---|---|
| Does a buyer have a statutory right to reject defective goods? | Start with the national Consumer Guarantees Act 1993. Establish who sold the goods, in what capacity, what went wrong and when the buyer acted. The city of purchase alone adds little to that analysis. [Contracts and consumers](contracts-consumers.md) |
| Is an activity allowed at a particular site? | Identify the site, relevant council, operative bylaw or plan, map, date and any applicable consent. For example, s 145 confers a bylaw-making power on a territorial authority. To establish a particular prohibition, locate the bylaw made under the applicable power and read its geographical scope and exceptions. [Local Government Act 2002, s 145](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2002/0084/latest/DLM172995.html) · [Land and environment](land-environment.md) |
| Who can deal with a Māori land or Treaty claim? | Establish the land's legal status, the claimant, the act challenged, any relevant iwi or hapū settlement legislation, and the order sought. An application about succession to Māori land and a claim concerning Crown conduct require different jurisdictional inquiries. [Māori land, tikanga and Te Tiriti](land-environment.md) |
| Can a New Zealand claimant sue or collect in Australia? | Identify where proceedings started, where the defendant is to be served, who issued any judgment, and where enforcement is sought. For service in Australia of New Zealand proceedings or registration in New Zealand of an Australian judgment, start with the New Zealand Trans-Tasman Proceedings Act 2010. For the reverse direction, check the corresponding Australian law. [Proceedings and enforcement](proceedings.md) · [Australian guide](../australia/index.md) |

Some questions need two guides. For an injury at work, use the private law guide to check cover under the Accident Compensation Act 2001 and the effect on a damages claim. Use the employment guide for review of an Accident Compensation Corporation (ACC) decision, a safety investigation or a grievance about a subsequent dismissal. For legislation versions, judgment paragraphs and subsequent cases, use the [New Zealand research guide](search.md).

## What weight does a judgment carry?

Read the orders and the reasons supporting the proposition cited. Locate the deciding court in the District Court, High Court, Court of Appeal and Supreme Court hierarchy, or identify the appeal route for the particular specialist body. A Supreme Court decision may have a majority on the outcome and agreement among all judges on a narrower legal question. For older Privy Council decisions, identify whether the appeal came from New Zealand and how later New Zealand courts have treated it. The date of the underlying New Zealand decision matters to the transition to the Supreme Court.
