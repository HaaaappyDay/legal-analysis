# Tax, KiwiSaver and New Zealand Superannuation

| Question | Facts and authorities to verify |
|---|---|
| Which income is taxable in New Zealand? | Fix the income year and taxpayer. For an individual, reconstruct travel dates and ties to any New Zealand home. Under the Income Tax Act 2007, s YD 1, a permanent place of abode in New Zealand can establish residence even if the person also lives abroad. The alternative presence test looks for more than 183 days in any 12-month period and can backdate residence to the first counted day. For qualifying non-resident visitors, check s YD 1B and the separate 275-day limit within 18 months. For departure, check the continuing place of abode and the absence test. Then verify income source, any transitional-resident exemption, and treaty residence or relief. [Income Tax Act, subpart YD](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/parts/DLM1512389/DLM1522892) · [Inland Revenue on individual tax residence](https://www.ird.govt.nz/international-tax/individuals/tax-residency-status-for-individuals) |
| Does a business need to register for or charge GST? | Identify the supplier, taxable activity and supplies, consideration, place and time of supply, and taxable turnover over the relevant past or expected 12 months. The Goods and Services Tax Act 1985, ss 6, 8 and 51, addresses different steps. For cross-border supplies, exempt supplies or a claimed zero rate, verify the provision for that particular transaction. [Goods and Services Tax Act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1985/0141/latest/whole.html) · [Inland Revenue registration guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/registering-for-gst) |
| How is an assessment disputed? | Identify who issued the first document and whether it is a self-assessment, a Commissioner assessment, a notice of proposed adjustment (NOPA), or a challenge notice. For a taxpayer-initiated dispute the ordinary NOPA period is four months, with its start depending on the document. A taxpayer disputing the Commissioner's NOPA ordinarily has two months from issue to send a notice of response. Track the later notices, conference, statements of position and applicable late-notice grounds under the Tax Administration Act 1994, Part 4A, especially ss 89AB, 89D–89DA, 89G and 89K. [Tax Administration Act, Part 4A](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1994/0166/latest/LMS835595.html) · [Taxpayer-initiated disputes](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/disputes/when-you-start-the-disputes-process/how-the-taxpayer-initiated-disputes-process-works) · [Commissioner-initiated disputes](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/disputes/when-we-start-the-disputes-process/how-commissioner-initiated-disputes-work) |
| When can a court or authority hear the challenge? | Check Part 8A of the Tax Administration Act, including s 138B, which assessment or decision is being challenged, and the issue date of the applicable notice. The ordinary challenge period described by Inland Revenue is two months. The Taxation and Charities Review Authority hears tax disputes, and the High Court is also a hearing authority. Check what issues were preserved and whether an exception permits an earlier challenge. [Ministry of Justice tribunal guide](https://www.justice.govt.nz/tribunals/taxation-and-charities-review-authority/) · [Inland Revenue on the challenge stage](https://www.ird.govt.nz/managing-my-tax/disputes/when-you-start-the-disputes-process/how-the-taxpayer-initiated-disputes-process-works) |
| Is the issue a KiwiSaver contribution or a withdrawal? | For missing contributions, reconcile the pay date, gross pay, employee deduction, employer contribution, myIR and provider records. Check eligibility, any rate election, reduction or savings suspension, and the rate effective on payday. For a hardship withdrawal, identify whether Inland Revenue still holds the initial contributions or the scheme provider holds the funds. Verify the decision-maker, evidence and amount available under the KiwiSaver Act 2006, Schedule 1, cls 10, 11 and 13. Other withdrawal grounds have different tests. [Contribution changes by date](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver-changes) · [Tracking contributions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-individuals/growing-my-kiwisaver-account/tracking-my-kiwisaver-contributions) · [KiwiSaver scheme rules](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2006/40/en/latest/sections/DLM378372/DLM379487) · [Hardship application route](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-for-individuals/getting-my-kiwisaver-funds-early/getting-my-kiwisaver-funds-for-significant-financial-hardship) |
| Who qualifies for NZ Super? | This public pension is administered by Work and Income. Check age, qualifying immigration status, ordinary residence at application and periods both resident and present since ages 20 and 50. Under the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001, ss 7–8A, the required total depends on date of birth. Check Realm-country periods, any social security agreement and special rules for refugees or protected persons. A KiwiSaver balance gives no evidence of how many residence years qualify. For a refusal, identify the review route in [employment and public services](employment-services.md). [NZ Super legislation](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2001/84/en/latest/) · [Work and Income eligibility and cohort table](https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/eligibility/seniors/superannuation/who-can-get-it) |

**Citation verification example.** Someone born on 4 July 1961 turns 65 in July 2026 and asks whether ten years in New Zealand qualifies them for NZ Super. Section 8 sets a 12-year total from age 20 for their birth cohort, including five years from age 50. Obtain the residence and travel history and check whether overseas periods count under an agreement before concluding that they qualify or fall short. Assess a request to access their KiwiSaver account under the scheme rules separately. [Work and Income eligibility table and overseas residence rules](https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/eligibility/seniors/superannuation/who-can-get-it)
