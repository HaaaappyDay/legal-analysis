# Land, tenancies, family, succession and torts

Identify the property, the parties' relationship, the date of the event and the order sought. For an estate, record the date of death and any grant of administration. For an accident, record the place, the injury and each item of loss.

| Issue | Questions and authorities |
|---|---|
| Title, dealings and Māori land | Obtain the record of title and the instrument creating the interest. Check the effect of registration under s 51 of the *Land Transfer Act 2017* and the applicable exceptions in ss 52–56. Establish land status under s 129 of *Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993*. For Māori freehold land, examine the rules for the particular dealing, including Part 7 on alienation and s 113 on succession to beneficial interests. Identify any Māori Land Court order affecting the interest. [Land Transfer Act 2017](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2017/30/en/latest/) · [Te Ture Whenua Maori Act 1993](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/4/en/latest/) |
| Tenancy, repair and termination | Establish who signed the agreement, whether an owner shares the premises, and whether an exclusion or boarding-house rule applies. For repairs, use s 45(1)(b) for the landlord's maintenance duty. If the tenant paid a tradesperson, s 45(1)(d) requires evidence of reasonable expense, the cause and urgency of the disrepair or risk of injury to people or property, and notice or a reasonable attempt to notify the landlord. For termination, check the tenancy type, statutory ground, form, service, event-date notice period and any Tenancy Tribunal order needed for possession. [Residential Tenancies Act 1986, s 45](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1986/120/en/latest/sections/DLM95099/) |
| Separation and relationship property | Establish the type and duration of the relationship, any separation or dissolution date, agreements and the source of each asset. Check relationship and separate property under ss 8–10, equal sharing and exceptions under ss 11–14A, contracting-out formalities under s 21F and application periods under s 24. Dissolution has separate grounds, including the protection-order ground in s 39A of the *Family Proceedings Act 1980*. [Property (Relationships) Act 1976](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1976/166/en/latest/) · [Family Proceedings Act 1980](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1980/94/en/latest/) |
| Children | Establish guardianship, existing orders, the care or contact sought, any violence and the child's views. Sections 4–6 govern welfare, best interests and participation. Check s 48 for the scope of a parenting order. Address child support separately if the question concerns payments. [Care of Children Act 2004](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2004/90/en/latest/) |
| Wills, intestacy and claims against an estate | Identify the will, witnesses, testamentary capacity, assets passing by survivorship, and any Māori land interests. Check execution under s 11 and the High Court's validation power under s 14 of the *Wills Act 2007*. Check who can administer the estate and the s 77 distribution rules on intestacy. A claim for family provision requires an eligible claimant, grounds and a timely application under ss 3, 4 and 9 of the *Family Protection Act 1955*. [Wills Act 2007](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/36/en/latest/) · [Administration Act 1969](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1969/52/en/latest/) · [Family Protection Act 1955](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1955/88/en/latest/) |
| Negligence, injury and loss | Establish duty, breach, causation, recoverable loss and any limitation defence. For an injury in New Zealand, check statutory cover under s 20, the definition of personal injury in s 26, and any special cover category. Section 317 restricts independent damages proceedings arising from covered injury. Test the statutory exceptions and s 319 where exemplary damages are sought. Obtain the ACC decision if reviewing cover or entitlements, and identify the relevant review route under Part 5. [Accident Compensation Act 2001](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2001/49/en/latest/) · [Limitation Act 2010, including s 11](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2010/110/en/latest/) |

**Worked citation check.** A cyclist breaks a wrist and damages their bicycle in a collision. An answer cites s 317 to dismiss both claims because the wrist injury is covered by ACC. Section 317(1) supports the bar on compensatory damages arising from the covered injury. Section 317(7)(a) preserves the bar even if the cyclist never lodged a claim. Section 317(2)(a) preserves property-damage proceedings, so the bicycle claim calls for proof of fault, causation and repair cost or value. Record the original citation as supporting only the personal-injury conclusion. [Accident Compensation Act 2001, s 317](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2001/49/en/latest/sections/DLM103473/)
