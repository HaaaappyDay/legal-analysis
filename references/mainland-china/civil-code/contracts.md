# Book Three: Contracts (Articles 463–988)

First identify the legal consequence sought. The General Provisions address formation, validity, performance, preservation of contractual claims, the modification, transfer, and termination of contractual rights and obligations, and liability for breach. Nominate contracts have their own special rules. Quasi-contracts include management of another's affairs without a mandate and unjust enrichment. [Civil Code, Book Three](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html) · [Judicial interpretation of the General Provisions of the Book on Contracts](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/419382.html)

| Question | Facts to verify and corresponding rules |
|---|---|
| Whether a contract was formed or a particular term binds both parties | Check offer and acceptance, actual performance, and written or electronic evidence. For standard terms, separately check duties to draw attention to and explain them, incorporation into the contract, validity, and interpretation (Articles 496–498). For the validity of civil juristic acts, also check [General Provisions](general.md). |
| Whether continued performance, termination, or damages can be sought | Distinguish agreed and statutory grounds for termination, the manner of exercise, and consequences (Articles 562–566); for damages, separately check breach, causation, foreseeable loss, mitigation, and other requirements (Articles 577, 584, and 591). For liquidated damages and remedies under an earnest-money clause, check the agreed terms, whether the earnest money was actually paid, and which remedy the claimant may elect (Articles 585–588). |
| Who may claim against whom | Assignment of claims, transfer of obligations, accession to a debt, creditors' subrogation rights, and creditors' revocation rights have different conditions. Check who must be notified, whether consent was given, maturity, and facts affecting realization of the claim. For general guarantees and joint and several liability guarantees, separately verify the agreement, guarantee period, and exercise of rights. |
| Which type of nominate contract applies | Classify by the substance of performance: sale; supply of electricity, water, gas, or heat; gift; loan; guarantee; lease; finance lease; factoring; contracts for work; construction projects; carriage; technology; safekeeping; warehousing; mandate; property management services; commission agency; intermediation; or partnership. For construction projects, specifically check qualifications and acceptance; for leases, distinguish lease duration and repairs; for factoring, check receivables. For mixed transactions, explain which category of rules applies by reference to each issue. |
| Whether restitution or reimbursement of expenses is available without a contract | For management of another's affairs without a mandate, identify whose affairs were managed, whether the manager had a statutory or contractual duty to act, the beneficiary's actual wishes, and any necessary expenses (Articles 979–984). For unjust enrichment, check the benefit and corresponding loss, whether there was a legal basis for the benefit, and any statutory exceptions. If the benefit lacked a legal basis, establish whether the recipient knew or should have known this and whether the benefit still exists (Articles 985–988). Where only transfer records exist, verify the purpose of payment. |

**Review example** An answer states that “delivery one day late permits termination,” citing Article 563. The provision is relevant, but it is necessary to check whether a statutory ground exists, such as delay frustrating the purpose of the contract. If the asserted ground is failure to perform a principal obligation even after a demand, also verify the demand and reasonable period for performance. Where a termination clause also exists, identify Article 562 as a supplementary authority and explain that the agreed terms still need checking. [Civil Code, Articles 562–566](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html)

**Judicial practice | Allocating responsibility for late software delivery.** The People's Court Case Database lists this reference case as `2023-13-2-146-003`. The appellate case number is `（2019）最高法知民终433号` (Supreme People's Court, civil intellectual property appeal no. 433 of 2019). The parties separately developed the front and back ends, lacked jointly confirmed functional specifications and a project schedule, and the commissioning party changed personnel and added requirements. The appellate court upheld dismissal of the commissioning party's claims. If an answer finds the developer in breach solely from the delivery date, this case can illustrate the need to examine cooperation, change records, and causes of delay; questions involving clear acceptance criteria and stable requirements require separate comparison. The court applied the former Contract Law. The Civil Code provisions appear under 关联索引 (index of related authorities) as cross-references.
