# Book Five: Marriage and Family (Articles 1040–1118)

This Book covers general provisions, marriage, family relations, divorce, and adoption. First identify the status relationship and relief sought; assess property ownership, debts owed to third parties, and obligations toward children separately within the same dispute. [Civil Code, Book Five](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html) · [Interpretation of the Book on Marriage and Family (I)](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/282071.html) · [Interpretation of the Book on Marriage and Family (II)](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html)

| Issue | Facts and authorities to check |
|---|---|
| Formation, invalidity, or annulment of marriage | Check registration, age, kinship, and marital status. Verify the grounds and time limits for annulment separately for coercion and for a spouse's failure to disclose a serious illness truthfully to the other spouse before marriage registration (Articles 1052–1053). Assess marital status separately from repayment of contributions made during cohabitation. |
| Matrimonial property, separate property, and matrimonial debts | Check the source and date of acquisition of property, the intended recipient of gifts, and property agreements. For debts owed to third parties, identify the relevant facts, the evidence supporting them, and who bears the burden of proof. Check for joint signatures or ratification and whether the debt served ordinary household needs or joint production or business operations. See Articles 1062–1065. For real estate, also check [Rights in rem](property.md). |
| Divorce and financial consequences | Distinguish divorce by agreement and registration from divorce proceedings, corresponding to Articles 1076–1079. Check the separate conditions for division of common property, compensation for domestic work, financial assistance, and damages; for domestic violence, also check the Anti-Domestic Violence Law and personal safety protection order procedures. |
| Child support and care, visitation, duties to support parents and grandparents, and adoption | Distinguish arrangements for direct care, child support payments, and visitation; check age, actual wishes, and facts relevant to the best interests of minor children. For a parent's claim for support payments from an adult child, check whether the parent lacks the ability to work or faces financial hardship (Article 1067). For a grandparent's claim, check whether the grandchild can provide support and whether the grandparent's children have died or are unable to provide support (Article 1074). For adoption, separately check eligibility, genuine consent, registration, dissolution, and when the relationship was established; for historical relationships, read [Temporal application](temporal.md). |

**Review example** “Any loan taken out during marriage must also be repaid by the other spouse” lacks the facts required by Article 1064. The review should identify the borrower, amount, purpose, signature or ratification, and available evidence. If one spouse borrowed in their own name beyond ordinary household needs, the creditor must show that the loan was used for the spouses' shared living expenses or their joint production or business operations, or that both spouses intended to incur the debt. Assess the original answer against those facts. [Civil Code, Article 1064](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html)
