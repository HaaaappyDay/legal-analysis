# Constitutional matters, administrative regulation, and ecology and environment

First establish who made what decision affecting whom, and whether the user seeks to confirm powers, establish eligibility, require performance of a duty, revoke a decision, or obtain compensation. Locality, administrative level, source of authority, and procedural stage often change the conclusion. Identify the specific versions of national laws, administrative regulations, local regulations, and rules; verify local procedural requirements with the issuing or implementing authority. [National Database of Laws and Regulations](https://flk.npc.gov.cn/) · [Legislation Law](https://hubca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2023/art_617433c825534c34a1104d67a6254ba2.html)

| Issue | Sources and review focus |
|---|---|
| Fundamental rights, state institutions, and oversight | Check the relevant provisions of the Constitution, Legislation Law, organic laws, Electoral Law, Law on Supervision by Standing Committees of People's Congresses, Supervision Law, and other applicable instruments. Distinguish fundamental rights, institutional powers, specific statutory duties, and procedures available to individuals; for recording and review of normative instruments, check the instruments subject to review, receiving authority, and submission method. |
| State security, national defense, nationality, and entry and exit | Depending on the actor, locality, and conduct, check the corresponding norms on state security, state secrets, national defense and military service, nationality, exit and entry administration, and consular protection. Identify competent authorities, jurisdiction, and special procedures; distinguish duties, administrative consequences, and elements of criminal offenses. |
| Administrative licensing, penalties, compulsion, expropriation, and requisition | Separately verify powers, delegation or authorization, statutory grounds, procedure, service, deadlines, and remedies. Check fines, administrative detention, sealing-up and seizure, and compulsory administrative enforcement separately. Administrative guidance or publicity material cannot independently establish authority to impose penalties. [Administrative Penalty Law](https://www.119.gov.cn/gk/flfg/fl/2022/1045.shtml) · [Administrative Compulsion Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2025/art_c5b6bfc45a5e45ca91b4a1ad2a1d2d93.html) |
| Information disclosure, education and health, transport and construction, and public safety | Check the Regulations on Open Government Information and special norms on education, health, road traffic, construction, work safety, fire protection, emergency management, and related matters. Verify the recipient of an application, service or licensing conditions, and statutory response methods and deadlines. Assess administrative penalties, professional or sectoral discipline, and civil compensation separately. |
| Land, planning, agriculture, and natural resources | Check the relevant norms on land administration, urban and rural planning, rural land contracting, mineral resources, forests, water, and related matters, as well as local implementing rules. Identify the nature of rights, land use, approving or expropriating authority, notices, persons entitled to compensation, and compensation standards; for property ownership, also check the [Book on Rights in Rem](civil-code/property.md). |
| Ecology and environment, and public-interest remedies | According to the dates of conduct and harm, check the relevant Books and chapters of the Ecological and Environmental Code, the Civil Code's Book on Tort Liability, special norms, and supporting rules. Distinguish the parties entitled or authorized to act and the conditions for civil compensation, restoration, public-interest litigation, administrative enforcement, and criminal prosecution. Check whether the conduct continued across August 15, 2026, when the contract was performed, and whether the limitation period had already expired by that date. [Information on commencement of the Ecological and Environmental Code](https://legalinfo.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbzhfx/zt/2026zt0106/xxxcsthjfd20260817/yw20260817/202608/t20260817_538554.html) · [Provisions on the temporal application of the Ecological and Environmental Code](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/508001.html) |

**Review example** If an answer cites a city's service guide to say “I can receive a subsidy at this rate anywhere in the country,” the original citation covers only the stated locality and subject matter. Check higher-level policies, authorization, and the current implementing instruments for the user's locality; explain separately the basis for eligibility, amounts, and application procedures.
