# Market transactions, intellectual property, and economic regulation

First set out separately the civil rights, regulatory obligations, and procedures the user needs to determine. Whether a complaint is accepted, an administrative violation is established, and a consumer can recover compensation are three different propositions. Select provisions or decisions that directly support each one.

| Area | Research starting point | Citation appropriateness checks |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer transactions, products, food and medicines, e-commerce | Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests and its implementing regulations, Product Quality Law, Food Safety Law, Drug Administration Law, E-Commerce Law, and the Book on Contracts | Check the purpose of purchase, the status of sellers, producers, and platforms, product defects or fraud, the type of subject matter, and exceptions. Verify returns, compensation for actual loss, and punitive damages separately. |
| Competition, advertising, and prices | Anti-Monopoly Law, Anti-Unfair Competition Law, Advertising Law, Price Law | Distinguish the rules on monopoly agreements, abuse of a dominant market position, confusion, trade secrets, false or misleading commercial promotion, and other conduct. A lead reported in a complaint does not by itself establish entitlement to damages. Check the version applicable during the conduct. [Anti-Unfair Competition Law (2025 revision)](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/5/20/art_104_206437.html) |
| Copyright, patents, and trademarks | Copyright Law, Patent Law, Trademark Law, Anti-Unfair Competition Law, and supporting provisions | Check the work or sign, ownership of rights, category, protection period, territory, scope of patent claims, and defenses such as use permitted under statutory exceptions. Acquisition of rights, civil infringement, administrative grant and confirmation of rights, and criminal liability require different authorities. |
| Personal information, data, and networks | Personal Information Protection Law, Data Security Law, Cybersecurity Law, and supporting rules; also read [Personality rights](civil-code/personality.md) | Identify the personal information processor under the Personal Information Protection Law (the party that independently determines the purposes and methods of processing) and the individual to whom the information relates. Check the sensitivity of the information, the purpose and legal basis for processing, and any cross-border elements. If an answer claims that “consent is always required,” check Personal Information Protection Law Article 13; even where a statutory basis for processing exists, verify specific duties such as notice and necessity. [Personal Information Protection Law](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm) |
| Banking, payments, investment, and financial regulation | Commercial Bank Law, Anti-Money Laundering Law, foreign exchange and financial regulatory provisions; for financial products, also read [Company and commercial law](companies.md) | Distinguish authorization requirements, product characteristics, regulatory prohibitions, contractual validity, investor loss, and causation. Check the issuing authority, covered institutions, and regulatory rules in force at the time. |
| Public finance, taxation, tariffs, customs, foreign trade, and investment | Budget Law, Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, laws on specific taxes, Tariff Law, Customs Law, Foreign Trade Law, Foreign Investment Law, Export Control Law | Verify the taxpayer, nature of the transaction or income, relevant period, tax base, eligibility for preferential treatment, goods classification, and import or export dates. Local replies and annual policies must match the specific locality and period. |
| Procurement, tendering, industrial policy, and sectoral market entry | Government Procurement Law, Tendering and Bidding Law, sector-specific legislation, licensing requirements, and mandatory standards | Check the nature of funding, purchaser, project scope, mandatory standards, and notices; objections, complaints, administrative remedies, and civil disputes have distinct conditions and deadlines. |

**Review example** “The platform received an administrative penalty, so every purchaser is entitled to triple damages.” The penalty decision can establish the administrative findings it records. The review must still check the basis of the consumer's claim, the particular transaction, elements such as fraud, and the compensation calculation base. When relevant provisions of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests are found, state that they supply the missing basis for the civil claim. [National Database of Laws and Regulations](https://flk.npc.gov.cn/)
