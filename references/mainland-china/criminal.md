# Criminal law and criminal procedure

First determine whether the assessment concerns suspected criminal conduct, conditions for prosecution, conviction, sentencing, or procedural rights at a particular stage. Reporting an offense, arrest, prosecution, and conviction each have different factual foundations. Verify the Criminal Law, relevant amendments and their temporal application, legislative interpretations, judicial interpretations, and procedural norms. [National Database of Laws and Regulations](https://flk.npc.gov.cn/) · [Criminal Procedure Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2025/art_48b3b0c3f954448e996602a71fc6f878.html)

| Issue | Review points |
|---|---|
| General Provisions of the Criminal Law | Establish the time and place of conduct, jurisdiction, age and capacity for criminal responsibility, mental elements, grounds precluding unlawfulness, preparation, attempt or voluntary abandonment, and conditions for joint crimes and crimes committed by organizations. For cases spanning legislative amendments, identify when the conduct occurred, Criminal Law Article 12, and relevant interpretations; check prosecution limitation periods separately. |
| Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law | For the alleged offense, check the relevant chapter, article, paragraph, item, and judicial interpretations. Categories include crimes endangering state security, endangering public security, disrupting the socialist market economic order, infringing citizens' personal and democratic rights, infringing property, obstructing social administration, endangering national defense interests, embezzlement and bribery, dereliction of duty, and military personnel's violations of duties. For monetary or circumstantial thresholds, check what is measured, the locality, and the relevant date. |
| Charges and evidence | Assess the elements of the offense separately from evidence supporting the facts. Check burdens of proof in public and private prosecutions, admissibility and exclusion rules, and the standard of proof for conviction. If a civil decision or administrative finding is used to support a conclusion of guilt, identify the elements and evidence still requiring examination. [Criminal Procedure Law, Articles 51 and 55](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2025/art_48b3b0c3f954448e996602a71fc6f878.html) |
| Compulsory measures, defense, and trial procedure | Distinguish investigation, examination for prosecution, and trial; establish the person's procedural status, competent authority, application requirements, commencement of time limits, and grounds for extension. Separately check provisions on special procedures for minors and others, private prosecution, incidental civil actions in criminal proceedings, appeals, and procuratorial protests. [SPC interpretation of the Criminal Procedure Law](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/286491.html) |
| Sentencing and execution | Verify the offense, applicable statutory sentencing range, sentencing circumstances, and evidence. Voluntary surrender, meritorious service, recidivism, admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment, suspended sentences, and combined punishment for multiple offenses each have specific conditions. For execution of sentences, commutation, parole, and community correction, separately check the applicable norms and procedural stages. |

**Review example** “The company owes payment for goods, so the person in charge has already committed fraud.” The contract and payment evidence can establish the state of civil performance. The review should check the offense identified in the original answer, the alleged offender, how the property was obtained, and the corresponding mental elements. Where facts are insufficient, identify only what remains to be established; supplementary Criminal Law provisions can explain the elements but cannot supply missing factual evidence.
