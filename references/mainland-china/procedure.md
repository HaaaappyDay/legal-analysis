# Dispute resolution, procedure, and foreign-related matters

First fix the action the user intends to take, then establish the parties' status, receiving authority, procedural stage, document type, and method and date of service. Assess admissibility, procedural outcomes, and substantive claims separately. If facts establishing when a period begins are missing, explain the calculation method and conditions without yet giving a definite final date.

| Question | Verification path |
|---|---|
| Civil litigation and evidence | Check whether the matter falls within the courts' remit, jurisdiction, parties, filing, production of evidence, standards of proof, preservation measures, levels of proceedings, types of decision, and remedies. For appeals, distinguish judgments from rulings, and check the parties' domicile and service; verify commencement, expiry, and extensions of each deadline. Also check the judicial interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law and provisions on evidence in civil proceedings. [Civil Procedure Law (2023 amendment)](https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-6231.html) |
| Enforcement, objections, and retrial | Check the effective legal instrument, required performance, performance period, limitation period for applying for enforcement, and suspension and interruption. Distinguish objections to enforcement acts from third-party objections concerning the subject matter of enforcement and their corresponding subsequent remedies; separately verify who may apply for retrial, statutory grounds, and time limits. |
| Mediation, notarization, and commercial arbitration | Check the type of agreement or instrument, whether it is enforceable, and what confirmation is required. For commercial arbitration, first check the version of the Arbitration Law, a valid arbitration agreement, arbitrability, and the institution; then distinguish setting aside an award, applying for enforcement, and refusal of enforcement. For labor arbitration and mediation and arbitration of rural land contractual management disputes, check their respective special laws. [Information on the revision and commencement of the Arbitration Law](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/s6052/moe_838/202509/t20250915_1413498.html) · [Labor disputes](social.md) |
| Administrative reconsideration and administrative litigation | Check the administrative act, actor, scope of reviewable matters, applicant and respondent or defendant, whether reconsideration must precede litigation, time limits, jurisdiction, and type of claim. Article 23 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law lists circumstances requiring prior reconsideration. The reconsideration decision, original administrative act, and an authority's failure to act may raise different litigation issues. [Administrative Reconsideration Law](https://hunca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2024/art_719196b6d7e540dc8adbe8b009933b04.html) · [National Database of Laws and Regulations: search for the Administrative Litigation Law](https://flk.npc.gov.cn/) |
| State compensation and judicial assistance | For state compensation, first distinguish administrative compensation, criminal compensation, and related judicial compensation; check the authority liable to compensate, statutory grounds, claim procedure, damage, and applicable standards. For legal aid, judicial assistance, and administrative compensation for lawful acts, separately check eligibility and competent authorities. |
| Foreign-related and cross-jurisdictional matters | Separately check jurisdiction, governing law, ascertainment of law from outside the jurisdiction, service and taking of evidence, and recognition and enforcement of judgments or arbitral awards. Characterize each issue under the Law on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations; verify whether party choice of law is permitted and whether mandatory provisions apply. For matters involving Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, separately check the relevant rules and judicial assistance arrangements. When citing a treaty, check contracting parties, scope, reservations and declarations, and effective dates. [Law on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations](https://wb.flk.npc.gov.cn/flfg/PDF/28487fc272234eddb41ba40d04d48188.pdf) |

**Standards of proof** For facts requiring proof in a civil matter, first determine who bears the burden of proof. Article 108 of the judicial interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law addresses the high degree of probability required for ordinary civil facts and evidence in rebuttal; Article 109 makes separate provision for particular facts such as fraud, coercion, and malicious collusion. Thus, even if both the question and answer use “beyond reasonable doubt,” identify the procedure, facts requiring proof, and applicable provision. For criminal conviction, check [Criminal procedure](criminal.md). [Interpretation of the Civil Procedure Law, Articles 108–109](https://ipc.court.gov.cn/zh-cn/news/view-6228.html)

**Review example** The user asks, “I received an administrative fine decision yesterday. Must I seek reconsideration first?” The original answer cites only Administrative Penalty Law provisions on the power to impose penalties. Those citations can establish “who has power to impose the penalty.” For the remedy, add Administrative Reconsideration Law Article 23 and other relevant provisions; check whether the penalty was imposed on the spot, whether another prior-reconsideration requirement applies, and the notice of remedies and deadlines. The fine amount and service date alone do not determine all procedural conditions. [Administrative Reconsideration Law, Article 23](https://hunca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2024/art_719196b6d7e540dc8adbe8b009933b04.html)
