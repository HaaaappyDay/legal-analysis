# Tax and superannuation

Determine the jurisdiction by tax type, year, taxing authority and entity. Select the applicable law separately for personal income tax, GST, state transfer duty and land tax; the investment holder, trustee, employer, employee and fund beneficiary may also be different parties.

| Issue | Citation verification approach |
|---|---|
| Residency and cross-border taxation | Check the specific income year, residence and living arrangements, relevant residency tests, source of income and applicable tax treaty. Visa status, nationality and a single day count are only potentially relevant facts. Distinguish general guidance, public rulings and private rulings applicable only to a particular taxpayer. [ATO tax residency guide](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/coming-to-australia-or-going-overseas/your-tax-residency) |
| Transaction taxes, state taxes and annual thresholds | For GST, check taxable supplies, registration and exemptions; for property transactions, separately consult the duties and land tax legislation of the state where the land is situated. Verify valuation, transaction or holding dates, the buyer's status, and the year's thresholds and concessions. State calculators may indicate the method, but specific exemptions and tax amounts require applicable authority. [State and territory legislation databases](https://www.opc.gov.au/links/legislation-states-territories-and-new-zealand) |
| Assessments, objections and review | Establish whether the document received is an assessment, review decision or general correspondence; check what may be objected to, service dates, time limits, late applications and subsequent review. For federal tax objections, locate *Taxation Administration Act 1953* Part IVC and the provisions for the relevant tax. [ATO guidance on what can be objected to and time limits](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/if-you-disagree-with-an-ato-decision/object-to-a-decision/what-you-can-object-to-and-the-time-limits) |
| Employer superannuation contributions and death benefits | For contributions, check the employment relationship, earnings base, relevant period and rules then in force; for death benefits, examine fund rules, trustee powers, the validity of beneficiary nominations and tax status. A will alone cannot establish whom the fund will pay; if payment is made to the legal personal representative, then examine estate distribution. [ATO information on superannuation death benefits](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/super/withdrawing-and-using-your-super/superannuation-death-benefits) |

**Citation verification example.** The original answer infers from visa documents that “anyone in Australia for fewer than 183 days need not declare any foreign income”. Visa documents support only the status they record. During review, also examine the residency tests for the applicable year, the nature of the income, treaties or exemptions, and identify the missing facts about residence.
