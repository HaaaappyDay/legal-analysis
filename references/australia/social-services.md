# Social security, disability, health and education

| Field | Outcome sought and verification |
|---|---|
| Social security and Centrelink | Identify the specific payment, residence eligibility, income and assets, family relationships and payment period. Changes to future eligibility, recovery of past payments, waiver and write-off of debts are different decisions. Check the decision notice, internal review and ART jurisdiction. [ART guide to Centrelink review](https://www.art.gov.au/applying-review/centrelink) |
| NDIS and disability services | Distinguish access to the scheme, funding of a particular support, plan changes and complaints about a provider's service quality. Check the decision type, history of internal review, statutory conditions and notification date; an example of an individual receiving approved services can be used by analogy only on corresponding facts. [ART guide to NDIS review](https://www.art.gov.au/applying-review/national-disability-insurance-scheme) |
| Healthcare, consent and professional discipline | Distinguish claims for clinical negligence compensation, patient consent or substitute decision-making, professional registration and complaints according to the request; check the patient's location, capacity, the decision-maker's identity and applicable state law. For regulatory sanctions against practitioners, verify the national registration scheme and local implementation and complaints mechanisms. For compensation claims, also consult [torts and damages](private-law.md). [Health department information on the National Registration and Accreditation Scheme](https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/national-registration-and-accreditation-scheme) |
| Education, child protection and statutory guardianship | Identify whether the body concerned is a school, education authority, child protection agency or family court. For school discipline, state child protection orders and family law parenting orders, check powers, factual thresholds and review avenues separately. For reasonable adjustments for students with disabilities and complaints, also consult the *Disability Standards for Education 2005* and applicable anti-discrimination law. [Education department explanation of the Disability Standards for Education](https://www.education.gov.au/download/13620/explaining-disability-standards-education/26557/explaining-disability-standards-education/pdf) |

**Citation verification example.** “The regulator disciplined the doctor, so the patient is certain to receive compensation.” The sanction can support the professional findings recorded in the decision. Separately examine the civil cause of action, causation and loss; if the user seeks an investigation into service quality, check the responsible body and conditions for making a complaint.
