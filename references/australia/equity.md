# Equity, trusts and restitution

First determine whether the user seeks recognition of an interest in property, recovery of money, or an account of profits from a fiduciary. Find authority separately for the basis of the right, the defendant's capacity, applicable defences and the specific remedy.

| Issue | Citation verification approach |
|---|---|
| Creation of trusts, beneficiaries and administration | Examine the trust instrument, the date of creation or variation, the property and beneficial arrangements, the trustee's powers and the local Trustee Act. Verify separately the identities of the registered owner, trustee and beneficiaries; for testamentary trusts, also consult [estates and succession](private-law.md). [Supreme Court of Victoria Trusts, Equity and Probate List](https://www.supremecourt.vic.gov.au/areas/common-law-division/specialist-lists/trusts-equity-and-probate) |
| Fiduciary duties and third party liability | Establish whether the relationship gives rise to fiduciary duties, their scope, and the facts concerning conflicts of interest, profits or consent. If the original answer states that receipt of property alone entails liability, check the type of liability alleged and the third party's knowledge, and locate the majority reasons. [Farah Constructions v Say-Dee [2007] HCA 22](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/sites/default/files/eresources/2007/HCA/22.pdf) |
| Unjust enrichment and restitution | For mistaken payments, failure of basis or other restitutionary claims, find Australian cases establishing the basis of the claim, defences, the defendant's enrichment and the reason for the transfer. Evidence of a transfer or a benefit to the defendant alone may still leave the legal basis for restitution unestablished. If the recipient pleads change of position, compare the facts and reasons of the original case. [Australian Financial Services and Leasing v Hills Industries [2014] HCA 14](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/judgments/judgments-1998-current/australian-financial-services-and-leasing-pty-limited-v-hills-industries-limited) |
| Remedies and priority | An account of profits, equitable compensation, an injunction and recovery of specific property each have their own prerequisites. A claim to trace into substituted property or to priority over unsecured creditors requires an account of the movement of the property, the involvement of third parties and any insolvency proceedings. Jurisdictional or case allocation guidance can support “where to apply”; substantive claims require separate case law or statutory authority. |

**Citation verification example.** “The house is registered in A's name, so B cannot have a beneficial interest.” The register supports the position as to legal title. If B claims a beneficial interest, examine contributions, the parties' intentions, trust instruments, applicable cases and state law; if a third party has acquired an interest, priority must also be assessed.
